1.Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Aha! Brain Puzzles (the “Game”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you download and play it on an iPhone or iPad. The Game is developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us” or “our”), who decides how the information described here is used and is the data controller for the purposes of data protection law, such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy applies to the Game and to its pages on this website. It does not apply to the App Store, to iCloud or to the services of our partners, which have their own policies (linked in section 5). Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

The Game has no accounts: it never asks for your name, email address, phone number or password, and we do not run servers that store information about you.

2.1Notifications

If you agree, the Game can send a daily puzzle reminder. iOS asks for your permission first. Reminders are scheduled on your device itself: nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Turn them off in the Game’s settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

2.2Sharing a puzzle

“Challenge a friend” and “Ask someone for help” create a picture of the puzzle and open your device’s share sheet. You choose who receives it and through which app. We do not receive the picture, your message or the recipient.

2.3The “Who’s playing” setting

The Game does not ask your age at the start. In Settings → Who’s playing, a parent or guardian can choose “Under 13”. The Game then sends no statistics, never starts the video ad service and shows no video offers (see section 13).

3.How we use information

  • To run the Game and save your progress.
  • To deliver in-app purchases, prevent them from being given twice and restore them where possible.
  • To offer optional rewarded videos, and give you the reward when you watch one.
  • To understand how the Game is played (for example where players get stuck) and improve its levels and features.
  • To keep the Game secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6.Advertising and tracking

The Game shows only rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for free lamps. There are no banner ads and no videos you did not ask for. The videos are served by Google AdMob, which may request them from other advertising networks it works with (its “ad sources”). Google acts as an independent controller for some of this information; its policies linked above apply.

The video service is never started for players marked “Under 13”, and for everyone else only once videos are first offered (after 5 solved puzzles).

6.1Tracking and personalised ads: your choice

When you first open the Game, iOS asks whether to allow the Game to track you. If you allow it, Google AdMob and its ad sources may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you personalised videos, measure them and link your activity with other apps and websites for advertising. If you don’t allow it, the Game asks Google for non-personalised videos only, chosen from context, and never uses the identifier. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Version 1.0 of the Game never asked and showed non-personalised videos only.

6.2Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, Google’s consent form is shown before any video is requested, and your choices there apply. You can review your choices when the form is shown again.

12.Additional information for U.S. residents

Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws have the rights described in section 11 to know about, access, correct and delete their personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. In the past 12 months the Game has collected these categories: identifiers (a random app-installation identifier, IP address and, if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier), commercial information (records of in-app purchases), internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay events and interactions with videos), approximate geolocation derived from the IP address, from your device, for the purposes in section 3 and shared them only with the providers in section 5.

If you do not allow tracking, nothing is shared for that purpose.

You may use an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf; we may ask for proof of the authorisation.

13.Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store’s Kids category. When a parent or guardian chooses “Under 13” in Settings → Who’s playing, the Game collects no statistics, never starts the video ad service and shows no video offers, and sharing a puzzle needs a grown-up’s check first.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age set by law where you live). If you believe a child has given us personal information, for example by email, contact us at safaoz90@icloud.com and we will delete it.
