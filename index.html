Help and support

Stuck on a puzzle?

Tap Hint for a gentle nudge; a second hint shows the answer. You can also tap Ask someone for help to send the puzzle to a friend.

Bought something on another phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open the shop or Settings and tap Restore purchases. Stage packs, “Everything” and special stages come back; lamp packs are used up and don’t.

A purchase went wrong?

Payments are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Anything else

Write to safaoz90@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model and what happened.

Privacy

  • No account, no name, no email. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • If a grown-up chooses under 13 in Settings, the game sends nothing at all: no statistics and no ads.
  • For players 13 and older: anonymous play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase, without the advertising identifier) help us improve the puzzles, and optional rewarded videos come from Google AdMob. Videos are not personalised, and the game never tracks you or uses your advertising identifier.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; RevenueCat keeps track of what you bought so you can restore it. We never see payment details.
  • We don’t sell personal information.
