Cozy Jigsaw Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Cozy Jigsaw Puzzles (“the game”, “we”) is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the iPhone app collects, why, who receives it, and your choices. In short: no account, no name, no email, no tracking.

1. What stays on your phone

Your progress (solved puzzles, puzzles in progress, hints, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your phone. We don't receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Optional videos (Google AdMob)

  • When you choose to watch a short video for an extra hint, the video comes from Google AdMob. There are no banners and no videos you didn't ask for.
  • Videos are not personalised. The game never asks to track you, doesn't use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), and asks Google for non-personalised ads only.
  • To show, measure and pay for videos and to prevent fraud, Google receives device and network information (such as phone model, iOS version, IP address and approximate location from it, language) and how you interacted with the video. Google's policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • In the EU, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first, and your choices there apply.

3. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. RevenueCat keeps a record of what was bought, linked to a random identifier made by the app (not to your name), so that purchases can be restored. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location access.
  • No analytics or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. We don't sell personal information.

5. Children

The game is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Videos are optional; you can always play without them.
  • In the EU/UK, you can change your consent choices from the consent form when it is shown.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal information. Because we don't know who you are, please tell us the app version and roughly when you played, and we'll help as far as we can.

The puzzle photos are licensed from Unsplash and Pixabay; see the photo credits.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: safaoz90@icloud.com.
