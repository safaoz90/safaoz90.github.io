Messenger: Last Warning

A tower defense game with a story

Cute aliens are racing toward Earth, and you build towers to stop them. But one of them is trying to tell you something before it falls: “Help...”. Defend your base across four chapters, and find out what the aliens came to warn you about.

For iPhone.

Help and support

Stuck on a level?

Build near the bends of the path, where enemies stay in range longest, and upgrade your best towers with crystals. Boosters (Crystal Rush, Freeze, Overclock, Airstrike) help on the hardest waves.

Out of lives?

Lives grow back over time. You can also refill them with coins, or watch an optional video for one.

A purchase went wrong?

Payments are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Coins and the Starter Pack are used up in the game and are not restored on another phone.

Anything else

Write to safaoz90@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model and what happened.

Privacy

  • No account, no name, no email, no statistics. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Optional rewarded videos come from Google AdMob. They are not personalised, and the game never tracks you or uses your advertising identifier.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see payment details, and we don't sell personal information.
