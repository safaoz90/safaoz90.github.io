Last Warning: Tower Defense

A tower defense game with a story

Cute aliens are racing toward Earth, and you build towers to stop them. But one of them is trying to tell you something before it falls: “Help...”. Defend your base across four chapters, and find out what the aliens came to warn you about.

For iPhone.

Help and support

Stuck on a level?

Build near the bends of the path, where enemies stay in range longest, and upgrade your best towers with crystals. Boosters (Crystal Rush, Freeze, Overclock, Airstrike) help on the hardest waves.

Out of lives?

Lives grow back over time. You can also refill them with coins, or watch an optional video for one.

A purchase went wrong?

Payments are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your coins are kept with your progress: signed in to iCloud, they come back after a reinstall and on your other devices with the same Apple account.

Anything else

Write to support@wozzly.com. Please include your iPhone model and what happened.

Privacy

  • No account, no name, no email. Your progress is saved on your phone and in your own iCloud, which we never receive.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics (Google Firebase, without the advertising identifier) help us improve the levels.
  • Optional rewarded videos come from Google AdMob. They are personalised only if you allow tracking in Apple’s question; you can change it in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Meta’s SDK tells Meta when the game is installed and opened, and about purchases, so we can measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see payment details, and we don't sell personal information.
