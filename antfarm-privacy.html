Ant Farm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Ant Farm (“the game”, “we”) is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the iPhone app collects, why, who receives it, and your choices. In short: no account, no name, no email, no tracking.

1. Your progress: on your phone and in your iCloud

Your progress (levels, coins, lives, boosters, purchases and settings) is saved on your phone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage), so it comes back after a reinstall and follows you to your other devices. We don't receive it. Apple's policy applies to iCloud: apple.com/legal/privacy.

1a. How the game is played (Firebase Analytics)

  • To see which levels are too hard, how long they take and which features are used, the game sends Google Firebase Analytics events such as “level 12 started”, “level 12 won in 48 seconds”, “shelf full on level 12”, “booster used”, “video watched”. Each event carries the level number and counts like seconds or taps, never your name or anything you typed.
  • With it, Firebase receives device and app information: phone model, iOS version, language, country from the IP address, app version, and a random identifier for the install. The game uses the Firebase version without the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not ask to track you, and sends no advertising signals. The data is used only to understand and improve the game and is not sold.
  • Google's policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

1b. Purchases

Coins and the Starter Pack can be bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, name or email. The game keeps a record of each purchase on your phone (and in your iCloud, see 1) so it is never given twice, and it can restore your purchases through Apple.

2. Optional videos (Google AdMob)

  • When you choose to watch a short video for an extra life or an extra shelf spot, the video comes from Google AdMob. There are no banners and no videos you didn't ask for.
  • Videos are not personalised. The game never asks to track you, doesn't use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), and asks Google for non-personalised ads only.
  • To show, measure and pay for videos and to prevent fraud, Google receives device and network information (such as phone model, iOS version, IP address and approximate location from it, language) and how you interacted with the video. Google's policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • In the EU, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first, and your choices there apply.

3. Reminders

If you turn on notifications, the game can remind you when your lives are full again. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself: nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Turn them off in the game's Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

4. What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location access.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites, no advertising identifier. We don't sell personal information.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it is not in the App Store's Kids category.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Videos are optional; you can always play without them.
  • In the EU/UK, you can change your consent choices from the consent form when it is shown.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal information. Because we don't know who you are, please tell us the app version and roughly when you played, and we'll help as far as we can.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: safaoz90@icloud.com.
