Messenger: Last Warning

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Messenger: Last Warning (“the game”, “we”) is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the iPhone app collects, why, who receives it, and your choices. In short: no account, no name, no email, no statistics, no tracking.

1. Your progress stays on your phone

Your progress (levels and stars, coins, lives, boosters, purchases and settings) is saved on your phone only. We don't receive it, and deleting the app deletes it.

2. Purchases

Coins and the Starter Pack can be bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, name or email. The game keeps a record of each purchase on your phone so it is never given twice.

3. Optional videos (Google AdMob)

  • When you choose to watch a short video (for a life, a booster, double coins, carrying on after a loss, or the daily gift), the video comes from Google AdMob. There are no banners and no videos you didn't ask for.
  • Videos are not personalised. The game never asks to track you, doesn't use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), and asks Google for non-personalised ads only.
  • To show, measure and pay for videos and to prevent fraud, Google receives device and network information (such as phone model, iOS version, IP address and approximate location from it, language) and how you interacted with the video. Google's policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • In the EU, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first, and your choices there apply.

4. What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location access.
  • No play statistics or analytics are collected.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites, no advertising identifier. We don't sell personal information.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it is not in the App Store's Kids category.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Videos are optional; you can always play without them.
  • In the EU/UK, you can change your consent choices from the consent form when it is shown.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal information. We hold none about you; for what Google receives with a video, see Google's policy above.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: safaoz90@icloud.com.
