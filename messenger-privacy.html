1.Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Messenger: Last Warning (the “Game”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you download and play it on an iPhone or iPad. The Game is developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us” or “our”), who decides how the information described here is used and is the data controller for the purposes of data protection law, such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy applies to the Game and to its pages on this website. It does not apply to the App Store, to iCloud or to the services of our partners, which have their own policies (linked in section 5). Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

The Game has no accounts: it never asks for your name, email address, phone number or password, and we do not run servers that store information about you.

3.How we use information

  • To run the Game and save your progress (including across your devices through iCloud).
  • To deliver in-app purchases, prevent them from being given twice and restore them where possible.
  • To offer optional rewarded videos, and give you the reward when you watch one.
  • To understand how the Game is played (for example where players get stuck) and improve its levels and features.
  • To keep the Game secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4.Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: running the Game, saving your progress and delivering what you buy.

  • Legitimate interests: measuring and improving the Game with statistics that do not identify you by name and are not used for advertising, and showing non-personalised rewarded videos that keep the Game free.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records and responding to lawful requests where the law requires it.

6.Advertising and tracking

The Game shows only rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for a life, a booster, double coins, another try after a loss or the daily gift. There are no banner ads and no videos you did not ask for. The videos are served by Google AdMob, which may request them from other advertising networks it works with (its “ad sources”). Google acts as an independent controller for some of this information; its policies linked above apply.

6.1Apple’s tracking question and personalised ads

The first time the Game opens, current versions show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency question. Your answer decides the kind of videos you see:

  • Allow: Google and its partners may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) and information about your activity to show videos more relevant to you and to measure them. This counts as “tracking” under Apple’s rules.
  • Ask App Not to Track (or if you never answer): the IDFA is not available to the Game or its partners, and the Game asks Google for non-personalised ads only.

You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The Game plays exactly the same either way. Ads are limited to content suitable for the Game’s age rating. Apple’s SKAdNetwork may also tell advertisers, in aggregate and without identifying you, that an ad led to an app being installed.

6.2Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, Google’s consent form is shown before any video is requested, and your choices there apply. You can change them later from “Privacy choices” in the Game’s Settings.

Information sent between the Game and our partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
